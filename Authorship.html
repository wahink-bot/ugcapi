Inspiration of the UGC API Calculator

Our team was dedicated to simplifying complex academic compliance. This tool was designed to help fellow faculty members accurately calculate their Academic Performance Indicator (API) scores as per UGC regulations.

About the Project

The UGC API Calculator is a specialized utility designed for Assistant Professors, Associate Professors, and Professors in Indian Higher Education. It streamlines the Career Advancement Scheme (CAS) application process by automating complex weighted calculations.

Methodology

Based on the UGC Regulations on Minimum Qualifications for Appointment of Teachers and Other Academic Staff.
