📊 How Researcher Earnings Work

💵 Base Rates (Market Standard)

Per Read: ₹4

Per Download: ₹40

These are industry-standard rates based on current market values

📖 Earnings from Reads

Your Share: 60% of platform earnings

Threshold: 250 reads

Once your article reaches 250 reads, you become eligible for payout

⬇️ Earnings from Downloads

Your Share: 70% of platform earnings

Threshold: 30 downloads

Once your article reaches 30 downloads, you become eligible for payout

💡 Example Calculation

Article with 300 reads and 40 downloads:

  • Platform earnings from reads: 300 × ₹4 = $15.00
  • Your earnings from reads (60%): $15.00 × 60% = $9.00
  • Platform earnings from downloads: 40 × ₹40 = $20.00
  • Your earnings from downloads (70%): $20.00 × 70% = $14.00
  • Total Earnings: $23.00 ✅ Eligible for payout!
